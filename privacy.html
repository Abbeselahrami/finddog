Privacy Policy

App: Paw Pick — Dog Pick · Last updated: July 28, 2026

Paw Pick does not collect any personal data. It is a simple, offline puzzle game. We built it to be fun and private by default.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email, or address, and does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data. There are no advertisements and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs in the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your level progress is saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network Use

The app may download a small configuration file to adjust game settings. This request contains no personal information and no identifiers.

Tracking

Paw Pick does not track you. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not link any data with third-party data, and does not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

Paw Pick is rated 4+ and is safe for a general audience. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app introduces features that collect data (for example, optional advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
