Privacy Policy

App: DogDoku: Find Dog · Last updated: 17 August 2026

DogDoku: Find Dog is a free puzzle game supported by advertising. We do not ask you to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. This policy explains what our advertising and analytics providers collect so the app can stay free.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly. However, the third-party services listed below may collect the following on our behalf:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when asked.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, and general region.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, at country or city level. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • App usage events — for example when a level starts, is completed, or is restarted, and when an advertisement is shown.
  • Advertising interaction data — which ads were displayed and whether they were tapped.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — displays the advertisements that fund the app. How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase Analytics — tells us which levels players enjoy and where they get stuck, so we can improve the game. Firebase privacy information

Both are operated by Google. Their combined privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking and Your Choice

The first time you open the app, iOS asks whether you allow DogDoku: Find Dog to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

  • If you allow tracking, our advertising partner may use your advertising identifier to show you more relevant ads.
  • If you decline, the app still works exactly the same. You will simply see ads that are not personalised.

You can change your decision at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your level progress, score, streak, and settings are saved locally on your device so the game continues where you left off. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children's Privacy

DogDoku: Find Dog is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Retention

We do not operate our own servers and do not store your data ourselves. Any information collected is retained by Google in accordance with their own retention policies, described in the links above.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete information held about you, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold this information ourselves, such requests are best directed to Google using the links above. You may also contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and we will revise the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will be reflected before the affected version is released.
